## Slide 1 / Opening: The Crisis of Unverified Claims in Autonomous Systems

"Ladies and gentlemen, distinguished evaluators, and partners. In modern technology, we face an unprecedented crisis of trust. Every autonomous platform, AI agent, and edge system claims to be secure, resilient, and compliant. Yet, when deployed in critical environments, these claims routinely collapse under the weight of unverified assumptions. In software engineering, we have long accepted a dangerous premise: that we can build mission-critical systems where progress is declared rather than demonstrated. CASTÚO-SYSTEM™ was created to eradicate this premise."

---

## Slide 2: The Core Thesis — No Claim Without Provenance

"Our foundational philosophy is absolute: *No Claim Without Provenance, No AI Deployment Without Assurance, No External Claim Without Reproducible Evidence*. We do not ask our users, our partners, or regulators to take our word for granted. Instead, we are building an Evidence Operating System—**EvOS**—whose goal is to bind operational state, security controls and architectural capability to verifiable execution artifacts. That binding is a target: the current S-001A evidence has open provenance and reproducibility defects."

## Slide 3: The Architecture of Protected Innovation (`castuo-evidence`)

"When presenting an advanced, IP-heavy ecosystem to the market, founders face a classic dilemma: how do you prove your system works without exposing your proprietary source code? 

We solved this by establishing a strict architectural separation:
1. **The Private Core (`Castuo-system`)**: the canonical, private repository for code and operational documentation. (`castuo-evolution` is a prepared external surface, not the current governance authority.)
2. **The Public Evidence Fabric (`castuo-evidence`)**: Our public verification surface. 

Through `castuo-evidence`, any third-party evaluator can inspect a candidate scenario fixture (not yet linked to the published evidence object), validate schema v2.0 compliance, test our negative scenarios, and read the reproduction protocol without ever viewing a single line of proprietary backend logic."

---

## Slide 4: Engineering Rigor — The Claim Firewall & Fail-Closed Design

"How do we prevent over-claiming and grade inflation? Through our **Claim Firewall**. In CASTÚO-SYSTEM™, every operational action is subjected to automated gating. If evidence is missing, if integrity hashes mismatch, or if a concurrency conflict remains unresolved, our system does not issue a warning—it enforces a strict, fail-closed policy: `CLAIM DENY` and `PROMOTION BLOCKED`. 

Our current public baseline, S-001A, is a bounded local scenario. It does **not** yet prove offline continuity: its source commit is unverified, the runner does not execute the fixture's phases, and its output hashes are not reproducible (EVID-REANCHOR-001). We explicitly state what our evidence proves—and equally important, what it does not yet prove."
